# Secondary Dominants in the Minor Mode

A practice page for writing secondary dominant chords in the minor mode on a grand staff. A separate drill covers the major mode.

Each example shows a minor-key signature, without naming the key, and a chord symbol beneath the staff: V or V7 of III, iv, v, VI or VII; vii° or vii°7 of iv or v; or vii° or viiø7 of III, VI or VII. Chords appear in any inversion except V6/4.

Click a line or space to place a whole note, then choose its accidental (double flat, flat, natural, sharp or double sharp). Wrong notes and accidentals turn red. A wrong note disappears when you place the next one. Start with the bass note: the lowest note must match the inversion. When every chord tone is in place, **Next example** loads a new chord.

Everything runs in the browser from `index.html`. Notation is drawn with [VexFlow](https://www.vexflow.com/).
